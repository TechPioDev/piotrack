## Architecture delivered

- **Data model** (9 tenant-scoped tables): `scoring_rules`, `intent_signals`, `alert_rules`,
  `sales_alerts`, `booking_pages`, `bookings`, `sales_assets`, `sales_plays`, `target_accounts` — all
  `BelongsToTenant`; `Contact::intentSignals()` relation added.
- **`LeadScoringService`** — sums matched active `ScoringRule` points (operators equals/contains/gte/
  is_true over lifecycle/source/title/opt-in/company/intent attributes); temperature hot>=60/warm>=30/
  cold; `apply()` promotes lifecycle to `sql` at the 50 threshold; `recomputeAll()`.
- **`IntentService`** — records signals, scores a 30-day window, high-intent>=20, `nextAction()` heuristic.
- **`AlertService`** — evaluates active `AlertRule`s, fires deduped `SalesAlert` + `SalesAlertNotification`
  to owners (score_threshold + high_intent evaluate today).
- **`BookingService`** — public `book()` dedupes Contact by email, creates Booking + meeting Activity,
  assigns owner (fixed or round-robin = least-loaded active member); reschedule / setStatus.
- **`AccountService`** — account score = company contacts' lead_score + intent; buying committee.
- **Notifications** `SalesAlertNotification`, `BookingReminderNotification`; **command**
  `sales:send-booking-reminders` (daily 08:00, next-24h bookings) via the per-tenant scheduler.
- **RBAC + entitlement**: 6 `sales.*` permissions (view / scoring / alerts / booking / enablement /
  accounts) mapped across roles; module gated by `entitlement:sales` (new `Feature::Sales`, Professional+).
- **Controllers + routes** (`/sales/*`, `can:`- + entitlement-gated, tenant-scoped) + public
  `GET/POST /b/{slug}` (resolves org by slug, CSRF-exempt, unauthenticated) + audit events.
- **UI**: sidebar **Sales** group; 7 Inertia pages (dashboard, scoring, intent, alerts, booking,
  enablement, accounts) with temperature badges, rule dialogs, alerts inbox, booking-page publish +
  copyable public links, asset library, target-account tiers; public booking Blade page.
